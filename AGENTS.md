# Insula Paper analysis rules

- Any future change to the currently approved analysis or figure conventions, or any deviation from the original insula-functional reference, however small, requires the user's review before implementation. Present the exact proposed difference and affected outputs first. Existing explicit approvals remain valid; do not infer approval for additional methods from a request to refresh data or redraw figures. The current approved exceptions are recorded in docs/MAIN_ANALYSIS.md.
- Group HGA means and SEM use electrodes as the statistical unit, with one waveform per bipolar site; do not average sites within participants before the group mean. PCA embedding follows the original per-PC explained-variance >5% rule, not cumulative 90% variance. The user has approved the current phase windows.
- Main analyses use **Repeat + sound only**, including responsive-site selection, Figure 1 HGA and significance counts, NMF discovery inputs, and dependent main-analysis figures.
- Never pool image/picture or text into the main selection or discovery waveform. Those conditions are separate modality comparisons; they cannot add a site to the main sound-responsive cohort.
- Use `src/main_analysis.py` for conditions, windows, cohort paths and palettes. Read `docs/MAIN_ANALYSIS.md` before changing them. A task named PictureNaming contains auditory trials too; select rows by modality, not by task name.
- Anatomical sampling and statistical responsiveness are separate steps. Keep unresolved anatomy and missing statistics explicit; do not treat missing evidence as negative.
- Retain zero endpoints whose native aparc, aseg and ribbon are all Unknown/unassigned, as explicitly requested by the user. Flag them as accepted Unknown, never as confirmed white matter. Insular midpoint and identity checks still apply; known CSF/noninsula gray matter and coordinate/version conflicts retain their existing decisions. Hammers-zero/FreeSurfer-insula disagreements remain separately pending.
- Use the historical ROI palette, especially SMC `#F39B35`. Anatomical ROI colors and NMF component colors are separate conventions.
- Preserve the original insula-functional Figure 1/2 plotting conventions. Heatmaps must blank saved mask=False points and order rows by first significant time separately within each phase. Restore original axes, scales, metrics, palettes and layout; do not redesign panels during a data refresh. Missing masks must stay explicitly unobserved and blank.
- Run processing, model fitting, plotting and tests via Slurm. Use existing environments, and export figures as SVG only. Keep outputs in this `/cwork` worktree; original insula-functional inputs are read-only.
- Figures 3–6 refresh is approved: freeze the accepted Figure 1/2 cohort and NMF; preserve original downstream tests, windows, plotting and correction families. Figure 5 models use current anatomically accepted task-specific Repeat+sound sites without requiring HGA significance; functional summaries intersect current assignments. Its fast/slow HGA uses electrode means ± SEM, RT subject-level inference stays original, and Go is retained. Figure 6 uses current three-phase fixed-H templates and recomputes projection and surface caches. Further deviations require review.
