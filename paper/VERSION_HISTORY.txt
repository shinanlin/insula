Manuscript version history

Versioning convention
Each saved revision has a matching manuscript_vNNN.docx and manuscript_vNNN.txt.
The Word document is the editable manuscript; the UTF-8 text companion makes
wording changes reviewable in Git. Paired ** markers in the text preserve bold
formatting. Blank lines separate manuscript paragraphs.

For subsequent revisions, retain earlier snapshots, increment the version
number (v002, v003, ...), synchronize the Word document and text companion,
and add a dated entry describing the changes. Commit the paired files and
this history together. Direct edits in Word are not automatically synchronized
to the text companion.
